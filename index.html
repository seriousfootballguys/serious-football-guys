COMMISSIONER'S RULEBOOKOFFICIAL
  1. Each team drafts blindly and picks one player for each position.
  2. A player can be rostered on multiple teams after the initial draft. On a bye, you cannot select a rostered player.
  3. There is no waiver wire.
  4. For an injury, the replacement player's stats count that week; that player stays until replaceable on a bye week.
  5. Bye-week replacements must be selected by midnight Wednesday of that game week.
  6. Adding a bye-week player drops the player on bye; the team is committed until another bye opportunity.
  7. A team may keep a player on bye and take zero points.
  8. Classic fantasy scoring. No PPR. Passing TD = 4 points.
  9. Every team makes the playoffs in Weeks 14–16; byes are based on standings.
  10. Weekly bonus = 10 points.

Scoring-engine note: the source rules do not define every classic-scoring value. The server therefore keeps those unspecified values in one editable scoring object.
